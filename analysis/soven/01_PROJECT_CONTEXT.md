# SOVEN project context

SOVEN helps people practise reading aloud, review recognized words and choose further practice. Its immediate engineering base is an English and Urdu classroom reading app. The confirmed product direction extends that base into an accessible storybook and voice-coaching platform for children and adults worldwide, targeting 70+ languages. This is planned, not implemented by the current two-track app.

## Product problem and positioning

Teachers cannot listen individually to every reading attempt, and learners need a calm way to repeat difficult words. SOVEN's current contribution is recorded reading, transcript comparison, practice suggestions and teacher visibility. A stronger future experience would put these actions inside a familiar book, with accessible controls and optional spoken help.

Suggested factual positioning: SOVEN makes reading practice easier to start and easier to review. A learner reads, sees how the recognized words compare with the sentence, and tries a focused exercise. Teachers can see attempts and progress in their classroom.

Do not turn this into a clinical or diagnostic promise. Dyslexia is not inferred from a microphone clip. Accommodation preferences can help users control presentation without requiring a diagnosis or disclosure. Population statistics, teacher time savings, learning gains and pilot adoption need sources or actual product trials before being used in a pitch.
