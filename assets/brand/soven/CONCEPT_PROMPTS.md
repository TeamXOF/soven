# SOVEN concept brief and construction

Three concepts were generated using the built-in image generation tool on 1 November 2026. Generated concept images are references; the final SVG is newly constructed with editable paths and text, not a bitmap embedded in SVG. No claim of trademark clearance is made.

## Shared generation brief

Professional identity for SOVEN, a global reading-practice platform. Exact uppercase wordmark SOVEN. A minimal S symbol combining a page and voice motif. Deep indigo, restrained teal, dark ink and warm white. Clean accessible typography, flat shapes, restrained geometry, clear at small size and in monochrome. No glow, detailed illustration, mockup, extra wording or ornamental gradients.

## Three concept directions

A: A folded-page S built from a strong ribbon-like silhouette, a small teal upper fold and a clear central negative-space opening. Pair with a confident geometric sans-serif SOVEN wordmark. Selected for the strongest small-size silhouette and monochrome legibility.

B: Explore a softer S formed by two opposing page folds with one subtle voice opening. Keep the symbol simple and the wordmark precisely SOVEN, with generous spacing and restrained indigo/teal contrast.

C: Explore an open-book/page S with a subtle voice/speech cue in the negative space. Use fewer geometric parts, balanced weight and a professional uppercase SOVEN wordmark.

## Final construction and evaluation

The selected mark uses two native SVG paths: an indigo S/page shape and a teal page fold. The wordmark is an editable text object. Light, dark and monochrome variants preserve the same silhouette. The quality sheet shows the mark at 16, 32 and 64 pixels and on light/dark backgrounds. Concept A was selected for readability, distinctiveness relative to the other two concepts, small-size clarity and single-color use. This is design selection within the explored set, not a legal distinctiveness assessment.

SVG text uses DM Sans with Segoe UI and Arial fallbacks. Install the licensed bundled DM Sans font for matching editable typography. PNG exports preserve their rendered appearance. Keep the SVG viewBox and breathing room intact; use the standalone icon rather than the full wordmark at favicon size. See analysis/soven/09_BRAND_AND_REPOSITORY.md for colors and usage.
